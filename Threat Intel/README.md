# Threat Intelligence Research

A collection of cybersecurity threat intelligence investigations focused on phishing analysis, malicious infrastructure, open-source intelligence (OSINT), and responsible abuse reporting.

## Research Areas

- Phishing website investigation and brand impersonation
- Domain, DNS, WHOIS, and RDAP analysis
- IP address, ASN, and hosting infrastructure attribution
- Threat intelligence enrichment and indicator analysis
- Phishing reporting, mitigation, and takedown tracking

## Investigation Reports

Investigation reports will be organized into individual case folders, containing technical findings, evidence references, and reporting outcomes.

## Research Ethics

All investigations are conducted for defensive security research and educational purposes. Indicators are defanged where appropriate, and public reports exclude sensitive or restricted information.

Findings distinguish verified observations from suspected malicious activity and unconfirmed outcomes.
