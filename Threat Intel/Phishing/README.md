# Phishing Threat Intelligence

This directory documents phishing investigations conducted through open-source intelligence (OSINT), infrastructure analysis, and responsible abuse reporting.

## Investigation Methodology

1. **Detection** — Identify suspicious URLs, domains, or phishing campaigns.
2. **Validation** — Examine available evidence to determine potential malicious activity.
3. **Infrastructure Analysis** — Investigate DNS records, WHOIS/RDAP registration data, IP addresses, hosting providers, and ASNs.
4. **Threat Intelligence Enrichment** — Correlate indicators with publicly available intelligence sources.
5. **Abuse Reporting** — Submit evidence-based reports to relevant hosting providers, domain registrars, and phishing protection services.
6. **Monitoring** — Track classification decisions, remediation responses, and possible takedowns.

## Investigation Reports

Individual cases will be organized into separate folders once their contents have been reviewed and cleared for publication.

All research is conducted for defensive cybersecurity and educational purposes. Public reports distinguish confirmed findings from unverified observations and exclude restricted information.

Potentially malicious URLs are defanged where appropriate. No unauthorized access or exploitation is intended.
